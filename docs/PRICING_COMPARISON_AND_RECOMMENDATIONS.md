## 💡 Pricing Psychology & Strategy

### **Why Unlimited Routes in Free Tier? (Key Competitive Advantage)**

**The Problem with Daily Limits:**
- ❌ Users hit the limit during planning sessions and get frustrated
- ❌ Creates artificial scarcity that feels restrictive
- ❌ Competitors (Kurviger, Calimoto) don't use daily limits
- ❌ Users might plan multiple routes in one session (trip planning)
- ❌ Feels like a "paywall" rather than a feature limitation

**The Solution: Feature-Based Limits (Like Competitors):**
- ✅ **Unlimited route calculations** = No frustration, users can explore freely
- ✅ **200km round trip limit** = Similar to Kurviger's 300km approach
- ✅ **10 saved routes** = Reasonable limit that encourages upgrade
- ✅ **Feature gating** = Users understand they're missing features, not hitting usage caps
- ✅ **Better UX** = Users can plan as many routes as they want, just with limited features

**Competitive Positioning:**
- **Kurviger Free:** Unlimited routes, 300km round trips, avoid roads, add POIs, limited saved routes
- **Calimoto Free:** Unlimited routes (1 region only), basic features
- **ScenicRoutes Free:** ✅ **Unlimited routes** (no region limits!), 300km round trips, avoid roads, add POIs, 15 saved routes, 3 curvature levels, strong social features

**Result:** ScenicRoutes free tier **matches or exceeds** competitors on all key features, providing strong motivation to use our product while still creating upgrade incentives through premium features (GPX export, offline maps, extra_curvy, route alternatives).

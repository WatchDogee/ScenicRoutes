## 📊 Executive Summary

This document outlines features and enhancements that would position ScenicRoutes as the leading route planning platform, ahead of Kurviger and Calimoto. The focus is on:

1. **Expanding Social Features** - Leveraging existing social strengths
2. **AI & Personalization** - Unique differentiators

4. **Competitive Parity** - Must-have features

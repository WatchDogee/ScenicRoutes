# How to Get Your Stripe Price IDs

## Quick Steps:

1. Go to https://dashboard.stripe.com/test/products
2. Click on your **Premium** product
3. You'll see the prices listed - click on each price to see its details
4. The **Price ID** is shown at the top (starts with `price_...`)
5. Copy each Price ID:
   - Premium Monthly Price ID
   - Premium Yearly Price ID
6. Click on your **Pro** product
7. Copy:
   - Pro Monthly Price ID
   - Pro Yearly Price ID

## Alternative: View All Prices

1. Go to **Products** → Click on a product
2. Under the product, you'll see "Pricing" section
3. Each price has a Price ID next to it (click to copy)

## What You Need:

- Premium Monthly: `price_...`
- Premium Yearly: `price_...`
- Pro Monthly: `price_...`
- Pro Yearly: `price_...`

Once you have these, we'll add them to your `.env` file!
